Privacy Policy

Claude Farsi Fonts & RTL

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Overview

Claude Farsi Fonts & RTL is a browser extension created to enhance Persian and Arabic typography and right-to-left (RTL) reading flow on claude.ai. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your data secure.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • No Personal Data Collected: The extension does not collect, track, transmit, or record any personal information, browsing history, user credentials, or conversation transcripts from Claude AI.
  • No External Transmission: No information leaves your device. The extension operates 100% locally in your browser.
  • Local Preferences Only: Your font choices, font size, line height, and RTL toggles are stored locally using standard chrome.storage.sync so your visual settings are saved between sessions.

3. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Used solely to save your typography preferences across browser tabs.
  • host_permissions: (https://claude.ai/*, https://*.claude.ai/*): Allows the extension to apply the custom open fonts (Vazirmatn, Estedad, etc.) and auto-RTL styling specifically on claude.ai.

4. Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party analytics, trackers, or external CDN services. All font files are bundled directly inside the extension.
